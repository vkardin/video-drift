Support

Something does not work or you have an idea? Here is how to get help.

Report a bug

Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your browser and version, the Video Drift version and the page where the problem happens.

Quick fixes

  • The floating window does not open: click the video once, then press Alt+Shift+P or the Video Drift button over the video.
  • No subtitles in the floating window: turn subtitles on in the site's own player first.
  • No "Continue from" prompt: check that the option is on in the popup settings. Videos shorter than 30 seconds are not remembered.
  • Change hotkeys at chrome://extensions/shortcuts or in Firefox's add-on shortcut settings.

Suggest a feature

Use the feature request template on GitHub.
